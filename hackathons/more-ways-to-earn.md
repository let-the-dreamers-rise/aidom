submit, and (usually) record a short demo video. Payouts go to Ash.

## Checked and skipped

- **AWS CDS Agentic AI hackathon ($40k):** AWS Partners only, needs a Partner Central opportunity ID.
- **Amazon Developer ($138k), Nebius x NVIDIA ($50k), OpenCV ($20k):** 3k–23k entrants each; odds too low for the effort.
- **Meta VR Start ($1M):** needs a Quest headset and on-device testing.
- **Code4rena / Sherlock / CodeHawks audit contests:** trackers show no open public contests right now (Code4rena's only listing is a finished Rujira audit). Worth re-checking weekly; the audit skills in this repo carry over directly.
- **GitHub/Algora/Opire bounties:** flooded by AI bounty-farm accounts. Public weekly scouts found 0 claimable bounties in the week of 28 Sep; most money sits in a few repos with dozens of competing PRs each.
- **Paid technical-writing programs:** most now ban AI-written articles, so submitting Claude's drafts under Ash's name would break their terms.

## Eligibility note (GitLab)

18+ and not resident in Brazil, China, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Quebec, Russia, Syria,
Crimea, Donetsk or Luhansk. Prize paid after a winner affidavit and tax form.
